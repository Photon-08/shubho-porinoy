A little backstory

আমাদের গল্প শুরু

Every great story begins somewhere. Here are a few pages from ours — আমাদের গল্পের কিছু ছোট্ট মুহূর্ত।

01

The first hello

How we met? It all began with a message about a college project at IIT Madras. She had a few questions about Business Data Management, and I had no idea that answering them would lead to endless conversations, countless little moments, and eventually, this beautiful beginning. From discussing a project to planning a wedding, we've come a long way. And now, we're ready for our favourite project yet — a lifetime together.. It was an ordinary day that quietly became one of our favourite memories.

The beginning
02

Somewhere along the way

Between long conversations, silly jokes, shared plans and cups of tea, we found a person who felt a little like home.

The becoming
03

She said what my heart had been waiting to say.

I was lucky enough that she found the courage I couldn't quite gather. In a story that began with endless conversations, it was Shaibalini who took the first step and expressed her feelings. I had been holding mine close, not quite brave enough to say them aloud. And then, just like that, she made it easier for both of us. What followed was the beginning of us — a journey of shared feelings, little moments, and dreams that slowly began to take shape together. Before long, we shared our story with our parents, bringing our two families into the journey too. Looking back, I'm still grateful she spoke first. Sometimes, all a love story needs is one person brave enough to say what the other is hoping to hear. No grand movie scene required — just a moment that felt unmistakably ours.

The yes
04

Now, the shaadi!

Now we're gathering our favourite people, planning all the little details, and getting ready to turn “you and me” into “us”. এই তো, আমাদের নতুন অধ্যায়ের শুরু।
